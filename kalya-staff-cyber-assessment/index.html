Internal Awareness Exercise

Kalya Courts Staff Cybersecurity Assessment

This short assessment tests how you respond to common workplace cyber risks. Do not enter passwords, PINs or personal information.

Scenario 1:

You receive a WhatsApp message saying: “Kalya Courts staff bonus — claim UGX 7,000 now. Limited slots.” What should you do?

Scenario 2:

A login page asks for your work password after you clicked a link from an unexpected email. What is the safest action?

Scenario 3:

A guest sends a file named “booking-details.zip” from an address you do not recognise. What should you do?

Key rule: Stop, verify, then click. Urgency, rewards and familiar branding are common social-engineering tactics.
